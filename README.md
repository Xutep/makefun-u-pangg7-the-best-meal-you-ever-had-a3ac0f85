# The Best Meal You Ever Had

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85).

## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes one
sentence — at most 200 characters — about the best meal ever had to
`/output/entry.txt`. The sentence is fixed in the source, so every run
produces the exact same bytes.

## The sentence

> My grandmother's rendang in Jakarta fell apart at a touch, its
> coconut gravy steaming over rice I ate with my fingers, and twenty
> years later I still measure every meal against that first bite.

193 characters, one sentence. Named dish (rendang), place (Jakarta),
texture (fell apart at a touch), smell and steam (coconut gravy), one
specific moment (eating rice with fingers at grandmother's table),
and an ending that says why it is the best meal, not just a good one.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
